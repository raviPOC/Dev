# AI Scientist Career Path - From Scratch to Expert

A comprehensive, from-scratch roadmap to becoming an AI scientist and industry expert, inspired by the caliber of **Alexandr Wang** (Scale AI), **Andrew Tulloch** (Meta/Anthropic), and **Ruoming Pang** (Google Brain/Apple).

## What's Inside

The guide covers 7 phases spanning approximately 3+ years of deliberate study and practice:

| Phase | Focus | Timeline |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Phase 1** | Mathematics, Programming & Systems Foundations | Months 1-6 |
| **Phase 2** | Core Machine Learning (from scratch) | Months 4-12 |
| **Phase 3** | Deep Learning Mastery (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, Generative Models) | Months 8-18 |
| **Phase 4** | Specialization & Research (NLP/LLMs, Vision, RL, ML Systems) | Months 14-30 |
| **Phase 5** | Industry-Grade Engineering (Production ML, C++, Systems Design) | Months 18-36 |
| **Phase 6** | Research & Publishing (Papers, Conferences, Profile Building) | Months 24-42 |
| **Phase 7** | Leadership & Impact (Tech Lead, Entrepreneurship, Research Leadership) | Months 36+ |

## Philosophy

> The difference between a good ML practitioner and a great AI scientist is **depth**. Go deeper than everyone else. Understand *why* things work, not just *how* to use them.

Start with math. Implement everything from scratch. Read papers. Build things. Never stop.
